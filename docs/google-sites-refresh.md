## About

I am an AI engineer and technical lead with over seven years of experience in artificial intelligence, data science and data engineering. Based in Lisbon, I work on the engineering foundations of reliable generative AI: agents, retrieval, context, memory, evaluation and deployment.

My experience combines work in enterprise technology and consulting with a background in higher education and scientific research. I hold a master's degree in bioinformatics and postgraduate qualifications in software engineering and data engineering.

I write engineering books that turn emerging research into structured methods, hands-on exercises and reproducible learning resources.
